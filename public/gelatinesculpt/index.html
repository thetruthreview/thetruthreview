What Is Gelatine Sculpt?

Gelatine Sculpt is a dietary supplement sold as a liquid with a dropper. The name suggests a jelly and the marketing calls it one, but the bottle is a tincture: you take a dropper by mouth and swallow it. There is nothing to chew and nothing to mix.

The printed label reads 1 fl oz (30 ml), 30 servings per container, and the two claims on the front panel are modest ones: it helps maintain healthy digestion and nutrient absorption and supports healthy metabolism. One detail worth knowing before it arrives: the printed label says 30 ml, while the bottle artwork the brand uses across its own pages reads 2 fl oz / 60 ml. Check which size is in the box.

Behind those sit two things — chromium, a trace mineral with an established Daily Value, and a 200 mg proprietary blend of twenty-two plant extracts and amino acids. It is made in the USA with globally sourced ingredients, in an FDA-registered facility.

What the Label Does and Does Not Tell You

Only the chromium carries an individual amount. Everything else sits inside a single 200 mg proprietary blend, so there is no way to know how much of each you are getting. That is common in this category and it is not a safety problem, but it is the honest limit on what anyone can say about the doses. Worth knowing too: the sales page builds its ingredient section around green tea, pure gelatine, turmeric and berberine — but gelatine, turmeric and berberine do not appear anywhere on the Supplement Facts panel. Green tea does, inside the blend. We go by the label.

Gelatine Sculpt FAQ

What is Gelatine Sculpt?

What are the ingredients in Gelatine Sculpt?

Each 1 ml serving contains chromium (as chromium picolinate) 0.7 mcg and a 200 mg proprietary blend. The label says to shake well before each use, take one or two full droppers daily, place it in the mouth and swallow.
