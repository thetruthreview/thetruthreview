Inside Every Capsule: 6 Targeted Ingredients

Every ingredient and its exact amount, straight from the Supplement Facts label.

🍃

Ginkgo Biloba Leaf Extract 4:1 · 100 mg

The classic memory botanical

Ginkgo (Ginkgo biloba) is one of the most widely used botanicals for memory and cognitive support. Its leaf extract is rich in flavonoids and terpenoids and has been studied for its role in supporting healthy circulation, including to the brain. [1]

🧡

Turmeric Root Extract 30:1 · 50 mg

Antioxidant support

Turmeric (Curcuma longa) owes its golden color to curcumin, a compound known for its antioxidant activity and its role in supporting a healthy inflammatory response. The 30:1 concentrate delivers more of the root in a small dose. [2]

🌼

Marigold Flower Extract · 25 mg

Standardized to 5% lutein

Marigold (Tagetes erecta) is a natural source of lutein, a carotenoid antioxidant that the body concentrates in the eyes and the brain. It is a popular nutrient in formulas for healthy aging.

🍵

Green Tea Leaf Extract · 15 mg

98% polyphenols, 50% EGCG

Green tea (Camellia sinensis) provides catechins such as EGCG, polyphenols known for their antioxidant properties. This highly purified extract adds green tea's signature compounds in a small, standardized amount. [3]

🧬

D-Glucosamine Sulfate · 250 mg

The largest ingredient by amount

Glucosamine is an amino sugar the body uses as a building block for its connective tissues. It is the ingredient present in the largest amount in each VapoCept capsule.

🎋

Bamboo Leaf Extract 30:1 · 5 mg

Standardized to 70% silica

Bamboo (Bambusa vulgaris) leaf is one of the richest plant sources of silica, a trace mineral found throughout the body's tissues.

Why These Ingredients Work Together

Each ingredient plays a different role. Ginkgo is the traditional botanical for memory and healthy circulation, turmeric and green tea add antioxidant compounds, and lutein from marigold is a carotenoid the body concentrates in the brain. Combined in one daily capsule, they deliver cognitive support in a format that's easy to stick with, and consistency is what matters most with any supplement.

Other ingredients: gelatin (bovine) capsule, magnesium stearate, microcrystalline cellulose.
