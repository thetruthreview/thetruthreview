What Is SodaTide?

SodaTide is a dietary supplement in vegetarian capsules. Each bottle holds 30 capsules — a 30-day supply, and the routine is one capsule with a glass of water, ideally in the morning. The bottle goes in the fridge after opening, which is what you'd expect from a product carrying live cultures.

Inside Every Capsule: Two Fibers, Three Strains

Both prebiotics are listed with their own milligram amount — straight from the Supplement Facts label.

🌿

Chicory Root Inulin 211 mg

The largest ingredient

Inulin is a prebiotic fiber from chicory root that your own enzymes cannot digest, so it travels intact to the colon and is fermented there by beneficial bacteria. In a randomized, placebo-controlled trial, inulin-type fructans improved appetite control in children with overweight and obesity. [1]

🥔

Potato Resistant Starch 100 mg

Starch that behaves like fiber

Resistant starch gets its name honestly: it resists digestion in the stomach and small intestine and reaches the colon, where fermentation produces short-chain fatty acids such as butyrate. In a randomized trial in adults with metabolic syndrome, resistant starch improved insulin sensitivity. [2]

🦠

Probiotic Blend 36 mg

Three strains, one of them notable

Bifidobacterium infantis, Clostridium butyricum and Akkermansia muciniphila. The third is the headline: in a randomized proof-of-concept trial in overweight and obese adults, daily A. muciniphila was well tolerated and improved insulin sensitivity and several metabolic markers. [3]

Why Pair Prebiotics with Probiotics

A plain probiotic sends bacteria into a gut that may not have much for them to eat. A plain prebiotic feeds whatever is already there. A synbiotic does both at once, which is the whole design here: 311 mg of fermentable fiber between the inulin and the resistant starch, plus the three strains that use it. The two fibers also work on different timelines — inulin ferments earlier in the colon, resistant starch further along — so the fuel is spread out rather than dumped in one place. The probiotics are grouped in a single 36 mg blend, so the individual strain amounts are not broken out, which is standard practice in this category.

Other ingredients: hypromellose (vegetarian capsule), microcrystalline cellulose, magnesium stearate, silica. Lactose-free, no stimulants. Made in the USA with globally sourced ingredients.
