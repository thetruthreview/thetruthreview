What Is MemoHoney?

MemoHoney is a dietary supplement in capsule form, developed to provide targeted nutritional support for daily mental clarity, sustained energy and overall cognitive well-being.

Its formula centers on L-arginine and L-citrulline, two amino acids the body uses to produce nitric oxide, which supports healthy blood flow. They are joined by niacin (vitamin B3), which contributes to normal energy metabolism, and beta-alanine.

MemoHoney is manufactured in the USA in an FDA-registered, GMP-certified facility, in vegetable capsules. You take just one capsule a day with a glass of water.

L-Arginine · 200 mg

Nitric oxide precursor

An amino acid the body uses to make nitric oxide, a molecule that helps blood vessels relax and supports healthy circulation, including to the brain. [1]

L-Arginine AKG 2:1 · 200 mg

Arginine + alpha-ketoglutarate

Arginine bound to alpha-ketoglutarate (AKG), a compound that takes part in the body's cellular energy cycle. Together with the base form, it brings the formula's arginine to 400 mg.

🔄

L-Citrulline HCl · 100 mg

Recycled into arginine

An amino acid the body converts back into arginine, helping keep nitric oxide production going and supporting steady blood flow.

⚙️

L-Citrulline Malate · 100 mg

Citrulline + malate

Citrulline paired with malate, a compound naturally involved in how cells produce energy. It adds a second form of citrulline to the formula.

🧠

Niacin (Vitamin B3) · 7.5 mg

47% of the Daily Value

An essential B vitamin the body uses to make NAD, a coenzyme at the heart of energy metabolism in every cell, including neurons. [2]

🛡️

Beta-Alanine · 40 mg

Carnosine building block

An amino acid the body uses to make carnosine, a compound with antioxidant properties found in muscle and brain tissue. [3]

Why These Ingredients Work Together

Arginine and citrulline work as a team: arginine is used to make nitric oxide, and citrulline is recycled back into arginine to keep that process going. Niacin supports the energy metabolism of every cell, and beta-alanine supplies the building block for carnosine. Combined in one daily capsule, they deliver nutritional support for the brain in a routine that's easy to stick with.

Also provides 18 mg of calcium (as calcium carbonate). Other ingredients: hypromellose (vegetable capsule), vegetable stearate, silicon dioxide.
