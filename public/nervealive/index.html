Inside Every Serving: Five Botanicals, Five Numbers

Each active with its own amount — no proprietary blend anywhere on the panel.

Passion Flower 145 mg

The lead botanical

The largest ingredient in the formula. Passiflora incarnata has been used for calm and sleep since long before supplements existed, and the US National Center for Complementary and Integrative Health keeps a summary of what the evidence does and does not show. [1] In a double-blind, placebo-controlled trial, passion flower tea improved subjective sleep quality in healthy adults. [2]

🌿

Marshmallow Root 110 mg

The soothing classic

Althaea officinalis — the plant the old confection was named after. Its root is rich in mucilage, and it has a long history in European herbalism as a soothing botanical. Second-largest ingredient here.

🏵️

Corydalis 100 mg

From Chinese herbalism

Corydalis yanhusuo is one of the better-known comfort herbs in traditional Chinese practice, where the tuber has been used for centuries. Its alkaloids have drawn genuine research interest in recent years.

🌵

Prickly Pear 50 mg, 20:1

A concentrated extract

Opuntia phaeacantha, the desert cactus fruit, at a 20:1 concentration — meaning 50 mg here stands in for roughly a gram of raw material. It contributes betalain pigments with antioxidant activity.

🌼

California Poppy 45 mg

The evening note

Eschscholzia californica is traditionally paired with passion flower in calming preparations, which is exactly how it is used here. It is also the reason this formula suits the evening better than the morning.

Five ingredients, five amounts, nothing pooled — which means you can compare this label against any competitor's, line by line.
