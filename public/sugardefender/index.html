Chromium 0.7 mcg

The one with its own number

The only ingredient listed separately, as chromium picolinate, at 2% of the Daily Value. Chromium is an essential trace mineral involved in how the body handles carbohydrate; the US National Institutes of Health keeps a fact sheet on what is and is not established about it. [1]

🍃

Gymnema Sylvestre

The classic of the category

Known in Ayurveda as gurmar — "sugar destroyer" — for the way chewing the leaf briefly dulls the taste of sweetness. It is the botanical most associated with this category, and the one most people recognise on the list.

🌿

Panax Ginseng

A long-studied adaptogen

One of the most researched botanicals there is. The US National Center for Complementary and Integrative Health maintains a summary of what the research does and does not support for Asian ginseng. [2]

🥭

African Mango

Fibre-rich seed extract

Irvingia gabonensis, a West African fruit whose seed extract became widely used in weight-management formulas. It sits alongside raspberry ketones and L-carnitine on the metabolic side of the blend.

🍵

Green Tea & Guarana

The natural caffeine

Two of the better-known plant sources of caffeine, which is a large part of why the brand says to take it in the morning. Green tea also brings catechin polyphenols, which the NCCIH covers in its own fact sheet. [3]

🏔️

Maca, Eleuthero & Astragalus

The energy trio

Three roots with long traditions of use for stamina and resilience — maca from the Peruvian Andes, eleuthero from Siberia and astragalus from Chinese herbalism. Maca is named first in the blend.

What are the ingredients in SugarDefender?
