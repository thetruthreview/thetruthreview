What Is HoneyHarmony?

HoneyHarmony is a dietary supplement sold as a liquid with a dropper, not as a capsule or tablet. You take one dropperful by mouth and swallow it. There is nothing to chew and nothing to mix.

The printed label reads 1 fl oz (30 ml), 30 servings per container, and the claim on the front panel is a single, careful one: blood sugar support, with the FDA disclaimer printed on the back.

Behind that sit two things — chromium, at 7 mcg or 20% of the Daily Value, and a 15.47 mg proprietary blend of twenty-one plant extracts and amino acids. It is made in the USA with globally sourced ingredients, in an FDA-registered facility, and distributed by Gex Corporation LLP of Ogden, Utah.

What are the ingredients in HoneyHarmony?

The label says to shake well before each use, take one dropperful daily, place it in the mouth and swallow. One dropperful is the 1 ml the Supplement Facts panel is built on, so the dose and the serving are the same thing — there is no arithmetic to do. Most people find the morning easiest.
