What Is Villora?

Villora is a dietary supplement sold as a Daily Gut Support Complex: one vegan capsule each morning, taken with a meal and a full glass of water. There is no powder to stir, no grit, and nothing to take again at night.

Each bottle holds 30 capsules — one month, one capsule at a time. Inside each one are eight gut actives, which the brand groups into three jobs it calls Sweep, Cleanse and Repair: six gel-forming fibers, one botanical and one probiotic strain.

It is made in the USA in a GMP-certified facility, the capsule is vegan with no gelatin, and the formula is non-GMO. The probiotic count is printed as a figure — 1.5 billion CFU, guaranteed at the time of manufacture — rather than described in words.

Inside Every Capsule: Eight Gut Actives, All Named

Six fibers, one botanical and one probiotic — every one printed on the label.

🌾

Psyllium Husk

Sweep · the best-studied fiber here

The fiber with the deepest research record for regularity, and the one the others are built around. It forms a soft gel with water, which is exactly why the label insists on a full glass rather than a sip. [2]

🥣

Oat Bran

Sweep · soluble fiber

Soft bulk that also feeds the friendly bacteria already living in your gut — a prebiotic and a fiber in the same ingredient. The oats your grandmother served, without the bowl.

🌱

Flax Seed

Sweep · the companion fiber

Works alongside the oat bran: more soluble fiber, more fuel for the bacteria that keep things balanced. One of the oldest foods in this category, used in kitchens for thousands of years.

🍏

Apple Pectin

Sweep · the gel former

The gelling fiber that gives apples their reputation. Here you get the pectin on its own, without the sugar that comes with eating six of them.

🍠

Konjac Root

Sweep · glucomannan

Its glucomannan fiber expands gently with water and is the most absorbent of the six. Prized in Japan for centuries, and the main reason the full glass of water is the one rule on the label.

🫐

Plum Powder

Sweep · the traditional one

Concentrated plum, the oldest remedy on this list and the one every grandmother already knew about — in a capsule rather than a bowl you have to chew through.

🥜

Green Black Walnut Hull

Cleanse · the botanical

The green hull, not the nut. Rich in tannins and juglone, and trusted by Greek, Roman, Native American and European herbalists for centuries. It is harvested green, in one short window each year — the reason the brand describes its batches as seasonal. This is the tree-nut ingredient, so read the allergen note below.

L. acidophilus — 1.5B CFU

Repair · the only one with a number

One of the best-known friendly strains, and the single component here that carries a measured figure: 1.5 billion CFU, guaranteed at the time of manufacture. That wording is the honest one — it is what the maker can actually stand behind. [3]

What is Villora?

Eight actives are named on the label. Six are fibers: psyllium husk, oat bran, flax seed, apple pectin, konjac root and plum powder. One is a botanical, green black walnut hull.
