What Is SlimTide?

SlimTide is a dietary supplement in vegetarian capsules, developed as a synbiotic: a combination of prebiotics and probiotics. Its main focus is to support the gut microbiome, aiding digestive health and metabolic pathways.

The prebiotics, chicory root inulin and potato resistant starch, are fibers that feed beneficial gut bacteria. The probiotic blend adds three strains: Bifidobacterium infantis, Clostridium butyricum and Akkermansia muciniphila.

SlimTide is manufactured in the USA in an FDA-registered, GMP-certified facility. It is lactose-free, and you take just one capsule a day.

Inside Every Capsule: 2 Prebiotics + 3 Probiotics

Every ingredient, straight from the Supplement Facts label.

🌾

Chicory Root Inulin · 211 mg

Prebiotic fiber

A soluble fiber from chicory root (Cichorium intybus) that the body doesn't digest. It reaches the colon intact, where it serves as food for beneficial bacteria such as bifidobacteria, and it helps you feel full. [1]

🥔

Potato Resistant Starch · 100 mg

Prebiotic fiber

A starch that "resists" digestion in the small intestine and ferments in the colon, feeding gut bacteria that produce short-chain fatty acids such as butyrate.

🦠

Bifidobacterium infantis

Probiotic strain

One of the best-known bifidobacteria, a family of beneficial bacteria naturally found in a healthy gut. [2]

🧪

Clostridium butyricum

Probiotic strain

A spore-forming probiotic named for butyrate, the short-chain fatty acid it produces and that the cells lining the colon use for energy.

✨

Akkermansia muciniphila

Next-generation probiotic

A bacterium that lives in the gut's mucus layer and has been linked in research to metabolic health, including a human study in overweight volunteers. [3]

🧊

Probiotic Blend · 36 mg

Keep it refrigerated

The three strains above are combined in a 36 mg blend. Like most live probiotics, the bottle should be refrigerated after opening to preserve their quality.

Why a Synbiotic Works Better Together

Probiotics are the beneficial bacteria; prebiotics are the fibers they feed on. By combining inulin and resistant starch with three complementary strains, SlimTide delivers both the bacteria and their fuel in the same capsule, so your gut gets daily support from both sides.

Other ingredients: hypromellose (vegetarian capsule), microcrystalline cellulose, magnesium stearate, silica.
