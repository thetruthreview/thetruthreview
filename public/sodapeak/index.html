Inside Every Gummy: 9 Botanicals in One Blend

An 82 mg proprietary blend in every gummy — about 567 mg of dry powders — straight from the Supplement Facts label.

🌱

Muira Puama listed first

The lead botanical

Named first on the blend — the position normally held by the largest component. Ptychopetalum olacoides is an Amazonian wood known in Brazil as "potency wood," and it has been used in traditional practice for stamina and drive for well over a century.

🏔️

Maca Extract

The Andean energy root

Maca (Lepidium meyenii) is a root vegetable grown high in the Peruvian Andes and used for centuries as a food for stamina and endurance. It is one of the more widely reviewed botanicals in men's wellness, with a substantial published literature on its composition and traditional uses. [1]

🌳

Catuaba Extract

Muira Puama's classic partner

Catuaba bark comes from the same Brazilian tradition as Muira Puama, and the two are so often paired that the combination is a standard of Amazonian herbalism. It is included here for the same vitality role.

🍵

Green Tea + caffeine 5 mg

A light lift, not a jolt

Green tea extract brings catechin polyphenols with antioxidant activity. The caffeine is the one component broken out by name on the label — and at 5 mg it is about a sixteenth of a cup of coffee, enough to add a little lift without the jitters.

🧘

Ashwagandha

The adaptogen for stress

Ashwagandha (Withania somnifera) is among the most studied adaptogens. The US National Center for Complementary and Integrative Health notes it has been studied for stress, sleep and related outcomes — which matters, because fatigue and poor sleep are often what drain daily drive in the first place. [2]

🩸

L-Arginine

Nitric oxide precursor

L-Arginine is an amino acid the body uses to produce nitric oxide, the molecule that signals blood vessels to relax and widen. That is the mechanism behind the formula's circulation angle.

🌿

Tribulus Terrestris

A men's-health staple

Tribulus is one of the most familiar names in men's supplements, used in both Ayurvedic and traditional Chinese practice. It sits on the vitality side of the blend alongside Muira Puama and Catuaba.

🍃

Horny Goat Weed

Traditional vitality herb

Epimedium, known as horny goat weed, has a long history in traditional Chinese herbalism for vitality and stamina. Its best-known compound is icariin, a flavonoid that has attracted research interest for its role in circulation.

Why These Ingredients Work Together

The formula is built in three layers rather than around one hero ingredient. Muira Puama, Maca, Catuaba, Tribulus and horny goat weed target vitality and drive. Note that the nine are combined in a single 82 mg proprietary blend, so the individual amounts are not broken out — the brand discloses the blend total and the caffeine, and states it is equivalent to roughly 567 mg of dry powders.
