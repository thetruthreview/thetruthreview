What Is KlaroNail?

KlaroNail is a topical spray for nails and skin. Instead of a pill, it is applied directly where it's needed: a fine mist that coats the nail, the cuticle and the surrounding skin evenly, including hard-to-reach spots.

Its formula is led by 5% undecylenic acid, a fatty acid derived from castor oil, combined with tea tree, clove bud, manuka, lavender and lemongrass oils and nourishing sweet almond, flaxseed, jojoba, chia and walnut oils, plus aloe vera and vitamin E.

KlaroNail is manufactured in the USA. Each 1 oz bottle lasts about a month.
